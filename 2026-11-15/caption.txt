What if Tokyo moved 820 km north? 🧭
もしも東京が820 km北にあったら？

Tokyo sits at 35.7°N: January averages 5.4°C and only 8 cm of snow falls a year (JMA). Move it 820 km north to Sapporo's latitude (43.1°N):
① January ≈ −3.2°C instead of 5.4°C (estimate, like Sapporo)
② Snowfall 8 cm → 479 cm a year, about 60×; snow on ~135 days (estimate)
③ Winter noon sun 30.9° → 23.5° (90 − latitude − 23.4) → your noon shadow grows from 1.7× to 2.3× your height
Then the Arctic Circle (66°33′N, ≈3,430 km north):
④ On the winter solstice the sun does not rise
⑤ On the summer solstice the sun does not set — the midnight sun
Lesson: latitude is sunlight. Earth's 23.4° tilt makes the seasons.
