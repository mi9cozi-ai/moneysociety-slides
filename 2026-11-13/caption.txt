½ gravity (4.9 m/s²)
① A 60 kg person weighs like 30 kg (weight = mass × g)
② Every jump goes 2× higher (height = v²/2g)
③ You switch from walking to running at ~1.4 m/s (estimate), not ~2.0 m/s — Japan's "80 m per minute" walk (1.33 m/s) is almost a jog
Moon gravity (1.62 m/s², about 1/6)
④ Jumps go about 6× higher; a phone dropped from 1.5 m falls for 1.36 s instead of 0.55 s
⑤ The same air weighs 1/6 → about 167 hPa at street level, like about 13 km up (estimate)
Lesson: gravity sets the size of everything — weight, jumps, walking, even the air.
